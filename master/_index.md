# Course introduction – School Physics – Lower and Upper Secondary {#kurseinstieg}

## Audience and prerequisites {#S1}

This bilingual self-study course progresses from basic lower-secondary investigations to upper-secondary physics. It assumes elementary arithmetic and willingness to read graphs. Advanced modules progressively require algebra, functions and other mathematical tools where relevant. The course is **not the official curriculum of any single German state**. Abitur content and optional enrichment are marked in the individual lessons.

## Learning goals and scope {#S2}

**24 modules · 149 lessons** in this English draft; equivalent German drafts exist for all lessons. Learners work towards designing questions, distinguishing models from observations, calculating quantities, discussing evidence and identifying applicability limits. The final five lessons teach Abitur study and assessment methods; they are not an extra compulsory content area.

## Suggested route {#S4}

Study M1–M12 as the foundation, revisit required mathematics and then choose M13–M22 according to your own upper-secondary programme. M23 offers **optional topics**; M24 practises exam reasoning and discussion. Before an actual Abitur examination, check your state-specific requirements.
