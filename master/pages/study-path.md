# Study path – School physics {#lernweg}

## Preparing to study {#S1}

Use the [Course introduction](../_index.md) to find modules. Ask three questions in each study block: What do I need to know first? What can I explain or calculate? What experiment or graph could test the claim? Record uncertainty and specialist terms in a study journal.

## Phases and transitions {#S2}

1. **Foundation (M1–M4):** [M1-L1](../M1-L1.md), measurement, units, simple motions and models. Move on when quantities and graphs are secure.
2. **Lower secondary (M5–M12):** forces, energy, electricity, magnetism, waves, heat and the other topics shown in the module list. Choose appropriate applications and supervised safe experiments.
3. **Upper-secondary bridge (M13–M15):** [M13-L1](../M13-L1.md), quantitative models, vectors and mathematical reasoning as structured in the course.
4. **Abitur-level study (M16–M22):** [M16-L1](../M16-L1.md), more complex models, experiments and evidence; check the indicated demand level in each lesson.
5. **Enrichment and practice:** [M23-L1](../M23-L1.md) according to interest and state curriculum, followed by [M24-L1](../M24-L1.md) through [M24-L5](../M24-L5.md) to practise interpreting, justifying and discussing.

## At each learning milestone {#S3}

Attempt at least one **unseen** question. Explain model assumptions and limits, check units and label invented datasets. Accuracy and understanding matter more than speed. Base revision on identifiable errors rather than on the number of lessons completed.
